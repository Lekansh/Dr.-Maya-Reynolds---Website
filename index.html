ABOUT DR. MAYA REYNOLDS

Meet Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD

Licensed Clinical Psychologist based in Santa Monica, California

I offer a warm, collaborative approach to therapy, combining practical tools with deeper reflection to help adults navigate anxiety, past experiences, and the pressures of everyday life.

Sessions are structured enough to feel supportive and goal-directed, while still leaving space for reflection, depth, and genuine connection. Together, we work to cultivate insight, resilience, and a stronger relationship with yourself over time.

Support for What You're Going Through

Anxiety and panic

Constant worry, physical tension, difficulty sleeping, or feeling like you are always bracing for something to go wrong. Together, we work to restore grounding and ease.

Trauma & past experiences

Single-incident trauma and long-standing patterns from childhood, relationships, or chronic stress, paced carefully with an emphasis on safety and regulation.

Burnout & perfectionism

Professional burnout, chronic stress, and high internal pressure. Develop more sustainable ways of living and working while reconnecting with yourself.

THERAPEUTIC APPROACH

Therapy That Meets You Where You Are

My approach integrates Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR, mindfulness-based practices, and body-oriented techniques.

Together, we work at a pace that prioritizes safety, understanding, and meaningful progress.

By understanding both the emotional and physiological sides of what you experience, I help you cultivate practical regulation tools and depth-oriented self-awareness that translate into your everyday life.

Support for What You're Going Through

Trauma & past experiences

Whether navigating a single-incident event or long-standing patterns from childhood, relationships, or chronic stress, I pace our work carefully, prioritizing emotional safety, stabilization, and regulation.

Anxiety and panic

Constant worry, physical tension, difficulty sleeping, or the sense that you are always bracing for something to go wrong. In therapy, I help you understand your triggers and build practical tools to restore calm.

Professional burnout

Support for entrepreneurs, creatives, and professionals who feel disconnected after years of pushing through stress. Therapy becomes a space to slow down, reconnect, and build sustainable ways of living and working.

Perfectionism & pressure

High-achieving, thoughtful, and self-aware individuals often carry intense inner expectations. I help you quiet internal judgment, soften overthinking, and develop genuine self-compassion and balance.

A Thoughtful Clinical Methodology

Therapy That Meets You Where You Are

Grounded in warmth and collaboration, sessions combine structured evidence-based interventions with depth and gentle pacing—safely bridging emotional healing with physiological regulation.

01 • Evidence-Based Care

Integrative Modalities

02 • Trauma & Nervous System

Safety & Stabilization
